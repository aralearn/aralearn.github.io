Privacy policy

Ara: Learn Georgian · last updated 26 November 2026

In short

Ara collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name (if you give one), lessons, review schedule, saved phrases, chosen language, voice and reminder time are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to any server of ours: the course, the phrasebook and all audio are inside the app and work offline.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics tools, or any third-party code that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your email, location, contacts, photos or microphone. The name on the welcome screen is optional and stays on your iPhone.
  • It sends nothing by itself. “Report a mistake” opens your own email app with a message about that phrase or exercise; nothing is sent unless you send it, and we see only what you send.

Reminders and calls

If you turn on a daily reminder, the iPhone shows it by itself; nothing leaves your device. The emergency button in the phrasebook opens your phone app to call 112; the app never sees the call.

The web version

Ara also works in a web browser at aralearn.github.io/app. It collects nothing either: your name (if you give one), lessons, review schedule, saved phrases, lists and settings are stored only in that browser, on that device, and are never sent to us. Clearing the browser's data for this site deletes them. Once opened, it keeps the course, phrases and audio on your device so it works offline. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the usual technical details of each visit (such as your IP address) to deliver the page; see GitHub's privacy statement. We receive none of it.

This website remembers only one thing: if you pick Light or Dark with the switch at the top, that choice is saved in your browser, on your device, and never sent anywhere.

Apple

Apple handles downloads, updates and, if you choose to share them, crash reports and TestFlight feedback. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy; we only see what Apple passes on (for example a crash report or the feedback you send).

Children

Ara collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does. Questions: ara.learn.app@gmail.com.
