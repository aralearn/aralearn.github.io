Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. The course, the phrasebook and the audio are all inside the app.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Can I change the language? Yes: Profile › Language (English or Polish). Georgian is spoken by one voice, Luka; you can hear him in Profile › Voice.

Is the voice a real person? The alphabet is: a native speaker's recording, changed into Luka's voice. Every other word and phrase is Luka, a synthetic voice. The app labels each one. More native recordings will come over time.

I already know some Georgian. On the first screens choose "I know a few letters" or "I can read Georgian", and the course starts further along. The phrasebook is always fully open.
