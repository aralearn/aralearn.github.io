Something not working, a word that looks wrong, or an idea?
Write to ara.learn.app@gmail.com. If you're testing with TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot in Ara and tap Share Beta Feedback.

Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. The course, the phrasebook and the audio are all inside the app.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Can I change the language or the voice? Yes: Profile › Language (English or Polish) and Profile › Voice (Eka or Giorgi).

Are the voices real people? Not yet: they are synthetic voices, and the app labels them so. Native recordings will replace them over time.

I already know some Georgian. On the first screens choose "I know a few letters" or "I can read Georgian", and the course starts further along. The phrasebook is always fully open.
