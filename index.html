Learn Georgian.
Use it today.

A colourful companion for people living in or visiting Georgia. Learn the alphabet and the language from zero, and use a phrasebook for real situations, offline, from day one.

Try Ara in your browser

Free, on any phone or computer: the whole course and the phrasebook, with audio. No account; your progress stays on your device. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and Ara works offline like an app.

What's in Ara

Two halves that work together: a course that starts at the first letter, and a phrasebook you can use the moment you land.

Learn

Start from the very first letter.

Georgian has its own alphabet: 33 letters, no capitals, one sound each. Ara teaches them one at a time, with audio and a tip for every sound.

  • 33 letters, each with its sound
  • Reading from the first lesson
  • Already reading Georgian? Start further along
Lessons

Short lessons that stay with you.

59 lessons in six stages, from the first letter to the dinner table. 15 kinds of exercise: listen, match pairs, spell, build a sentence, choose your reply.

  • Words you'll really use
  • Grammar in plain words
  • Tap any word to see its meaning
  • A wrong answer comes back at the end
Use in Georgia

302 phrases for real situations.

Ready from day one, with nothing to unlock: 11 situations, each with the words you need right now. Save a phrase and it joins your reviews.

  • Transport
  • Shopping & bazaar
  • Restaurants & cafés
  • Health & pharmacy
  • Home & everyday
  • Emergencies, with 112
Show mode

Too shy to say it? Show it.

One tap turns a phrase into big, clear Georgian to show a driver or a shopkeeper. Or play it aloud, slowly if you like.

  • Big text, light or dark
  • Audio, normal or slow
  • A sounds-like spelling to say it yourself
Conversations

Practise real conversations.

21 everyday dialogues: at the bazaar, on a marshrutka, at a friend's for dinner. Read along, hear every line, then take a side and answer yourself.

  • Taking a taxi
  • At the bazaar
  • At the pharmacy
  • Dinner at a friend's
  • Buying a SIM card
  • Calling 112
Words

Find any word, however you type it.

Search in English, in Georgian or in Latin letters, and typos are fine. Words, phrases, situations and conversations, all in one place.

  • Every word in the app, with its meaning
  • What you know and what's new
Review

Remember it next week, too.

Each word comes back just before you'd forget it: after a day, then three, then eight. Miss one and it returns in ten minutes.

  • A few minutes a day
  • The difficult words, gathered for you

And a few more things

Works offline

The course, the phrasebook and every audio clip are inside the app. No signal at the bazaar? No problem.

Nothing collected

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. Privacy policy

Two voices

Hear every word from Eka or Giorgi. They're synthetic voices, and Ara labels them so.

English or Polish

All of Ara in your language. For Polish speakers, sounds and grammar are compared with Polish.

Tap any word

Every Georgian word in examples, phrases and conversations explains itself with one tap.

Help in an emergency

Emergency phrases right at the top of the phrasebook, and a button that calls 112.
